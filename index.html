About Chaos

Emerging from the coastal city of Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, in 2005, Chaos has spent two decades cementing their status as one of India's most relentless & respected metal acts. Defined by a high-octane blend of old-school thrash aggression & modern "groove-metal" precision, the band has consistently pushed the boundaries of the Indian independent scene. The band has released three ful-length albums; Violent Redemption (2013), All Against All (2018) & Bane of Humanity (2026).
